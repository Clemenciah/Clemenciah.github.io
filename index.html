About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Centrum Wiskunde & Informatica (CWI) in Amsterdam. In January 2027, I will join the Web Information Systems (WIS) group at Delft University of Technology as an Assistant Professor.

My research lies at the intersection of Information Retrieval, Natural Language Processing, and Human–AI Interaction. I am interested in how we evaluate AI systems, whether existing evaluation methods reflect human needs and judgments, and how reliable these methods are across different tasks, languages, and contexts. My work spans human-centered evaluation, LLM-based evaluation, and responsible and trustworthy AI.

I received my PhD from the University of Amsterdam in July 2025, under the supervision of Maarten de Rijke and Mohammad Aliannejadi. My thesis is titled Rethinking the Human-Centered Evaluation of Conversational Systems. Before that, I completed my MSc at the African Institute for Mathematical Sciences (AMMI) in Rwanda and my BSc at Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology in Kenya.

I serve on the board of the Masakhane Research Foundation, which supports research on NLP for African languages. I also co-organize the AfricaNLP workshops.

Research Interests

  • Human-centered evaluation of AI systems
  • Evaluation of and with large language models
  • Conversational search and information retrieval
  • Multilingual and cross-cultural NLP, with a focus on African languages
  • Responsible and trustworthy AI
