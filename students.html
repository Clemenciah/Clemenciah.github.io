Prospective Students

I will start as an Assistant Professor in the Web Information Systems (WIS) group at Delft University of Technology in January 2027. I am interested in working with motivated students on human-centered evaluation of AI systems, evaluation of and with large language models, conversational search and information retrieval, multilingual NLP with a focus on African languages, and responsible and trustworthy AI.
